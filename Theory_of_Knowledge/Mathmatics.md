# Mathmatics

## Is math discovered or invented

### platonist perspective (discovery)
- thinks that there is an objective reality
- math exists outside mind and culture
- Evidence: universe without life is still a universe with mathematical rules and laws of physics.
- because math already exists, and it already exists as the fundamental rules of the universe, by using math, we can make valid predictions.

### formalist (invention)
- math as a language/tool
- Evidence: numbers only exist within our minds, geometry and abstract math
- using abstract ideas to make predictions may come close in patterns of reality, but it does not make the abstract ideas real
- it argues that math is true and logical but not real
