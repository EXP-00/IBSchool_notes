## The Five Kinematic Equations

For constant acceleration motion with variables:
- $u$ = initial velocity
- $v$ = final velocity
- $a$ = acceleration
- $t$ = time
- $s$ = displacement

### Equation 1 (missing $s$)
$$v = u + at$$

### Equation 2 (missing $v$)
$$s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$$

### Equation 3 (missing $t$)
$$v^2 = u^2 + 2as$$

### Equation 4 (missing $u$)
$$s = vt - \frac{1}{2}at^2$$

### Equation 5 (missing $a$)
$$s = \frac{u + v}{2}t$$
