# Uncertainties

## how do we determine uncertainties
Our instruments suck ass

Analogue instruments has half of its smallest unit of measurement as uncertainty +- 0.005

Digital instruments has its smallest unit as uncertainty +- 0.01

## uncertainty equations
when calculating equations, each variable's uncertainties should also be accounted

## absolute and relative uncertainties
absolute uncertainty is.... uncertainty. It should be directly added in arithmetic equations.

relative uncertainty is uncertainty out of total value. a fraction, a percentage of how much is uncertain compared to the whole.

relative uncertainty should be ADDED in geometric functions. first, calculate the individual variable's R-uncertainty, then add them. EVEN if its dividing.

## graphing uncertainties
just graph the greatest possible gradient and least possible gradient.

use the furthest data x value and smallest data x.
