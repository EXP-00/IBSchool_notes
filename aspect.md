
Aspects are hardmode variants of gem staves. Visors are hardmode variants of gem robes 

All aspects have their staves in their recipes. 

Aspects can be empowered by ONLY their respective gem robes. 

Aspects should have a primitive trail and have the appearance of two perpendicular sharp tears stacked on each other, same as vanilla staves which just got reworked recently. Reference drawprettysparckles which terrablade etc utilizes. 

Aspects have a crit of 10%, base rarity of pink. 

 

Aspect of amethyst now fire from a random radius away from the cursor and fires at the center, increased homing compared to amethyst staff. One pierce. It cannot home onto the same target twice. (use time 14, velo 6, damage 55) Upgraded with crystal shards and souls of light and a gelatinous crystal.  

Gem robe will add a second ring that fires at 30% speed 

 

Aspect of topaz now have a primed explosion, which it flies for 1 second before exploding for 100% damage. A pre-emptive hit will only deal 50% damage explosion and 50% radius. (use time 37, velo 9, damage 120) Upgraded with light shard x 2, souls of light, and pixie dust 

Gem robe will make the explosion radius 100% bigger. 

 

Aspect of Sapphire fires four consecutive gems each doing their own helix with random progress. (different x offsets in the sine wave.) (use time 4, animation 16, reuse delay 12, velo 8, damage 50) 

Upgraded with souls of night, sight, and tier three hardmode bars. 

Gem robe will increase the size of the helix, and add a mirrored projectile for each helix. Reduced 30% damage for each projectile. 

 

Aspect of emerald have fast projectile speed, and sticks to blocks, they have three pierce. There can be three maximum crystals that is stuck to blocks. When pierce depleted,  stuck emeralds burst into a pillar of emeralds with infinite pierce and 100% damage for a short time, immunity frames local 10. The pillars point outwards from the block attatched similiar to blood thorn(reference projectile ID 756). (use time 24, velo 12, 30 damage) 

Upgraded with bloodthorn and purification powder 

Gem robe increases the maximum crystals stuck to blocks to 8. 

 

Aspect of amber repeatedly bounce toward enemies like nano bullets, and have two pierce. (use time 24 velo 10, damage 60). This is the only aspect with gravity enabled. Reference chlorophyte arrows for ai behavior.  

Upgraded with desert spirit lamp and amber torch plus desert torch. 

Gem robe adds one pierce. 

 

Aspect of ruby spawn a volley of gems (3) at a random y position on the left side or the right side of the player’s screen (opposite side to where the player is aiming). No pierce, ignores blocks. (use time 5, animation 16, reuse delay 16, extra updates 1, velo 10 * 2, damage 60) 

 Upgraded with souls of might, night, and starfury. 

Gem robe adds another gem to the volley (usetime 5-> 4) 

 

Aspect of diamond can be charged for a maximum of 2 seconds. It does 300% damage, 200% size, and loses 50% speed when at max charge. It has the largest base hitbox of all the gems. It releases a visual white flash on impact.  (2x size, use time 25, velo 8, base damage 120) 

Upgraded with hallowed bars. 

Gem robe makes the maximum charge time 4 seconds and increases maximum damage to 800% and 250% size. Speed unchanged after 2 seconds. 

 

Amethyst visor provides great homing capabilities for all common mage staves. Frost, crystal serpent, sky fracture, gemstaves, inferno stave, meteor staff, poison staff. 

Topaz visor will add an explosion to all gem stave projectiles for 33% damage. 

Saphire visor will double the projectiles of all common staves at reduced 40% damage. Second projectile will have inaccuracy. 

 

Emerald visor make all other gemstaves erupt three gem pillars when hitting an enemy at the hit normal. All pillars have –1 immunity frames and the main pillar does 20% damage, while the other pillars does 10% damage. 

 

Amber staves give +1 pierce to all common staves, but make them deal 30% less damage every enemy hit. 

 

Ruby visor will increase time flow for all magic weapons (+1 extra update) 

 

Diamond visor will increase the size of all stave projectiles by 50%, decrease 33% use speed, and increase 33% damage. 

 

All visors are crafted via their respective giant gem and hallowed bars. They gain a set bonus with gem robes which decreases mana usage by 50%.
